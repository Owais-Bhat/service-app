STOCK - TECHNICIAN KO DENA, JOB PAR LAGANA, WAPAS LENA
---------------------------------------------------------------------
(Purchases & Stock -> Stock. Upar 3 naye button hain.)

1. DENA     : "Give to technician" -> technician chuno, "Take it from" me Main Store,
              item search karke likho aur quantity daalo -> "Give stock".
              (Pehli baar us technician ki van apne aap ban jaati hai.)
              Ye sale ya kharcha nahi hai - maal aapka hi hai, bas uske paas likha gaya.

2. LAGANA   : Installation / service par jo maal laga, wo "Used on a job" se likho:
              - Job: ticket number, customer ka naam ya phone likhkar dhoondho
              - "Used / fitted on the job" chuno (technician ki van apne aap aa jaati hai)
              - Item search karo, quantity, aur customer se kitna charge karna hai
              - "Record" dabao. Approval maange to "approve now" haan karo.
              Approve hote hi maal van se nikal jaata hai, cost accounts me jaati hai,
              aur charge job ke invoice me aa sakta hai.

3. WAPAS    : Jo maal technician ne use nahi kiya aur dukaan wapas laya:
              "Take back" -> technician chuno, item aur quantity -> "Take back".
              Jo device customer ki jagah se nikal kar wapas aaya (removed/unused):
              "Used on a job" me "Came back from the job" chuno.

4. SEARCH   : Ab har jagah item likhkar dhoondh sakte ho (naam, brand, model ya SKU ka
              koi bhi hissa) - stock update (Adjust), Give, Take back, Used on a job.
              Item ke saamne dikhta hai ki us jagah kitna hai.

 PART B - WHATSAPP TEMPLATES JO AAPKO BANANE HAIN
=====================================================================

Fast2SMS dashboard -> WhatsApp -> Templates -> New template.

Sabke liye:
  - Language        : English
  - Template name   : sirf chhote akshar aur underscore (jaise invoice_document)
  - Variables       : {{1}}, {{2}}... EXACT isi order me (order badalna mat -
                      portal isi order me bharta hai)
  - Sample values   : Meta approval ke liye har variable ka ek namuna bharna
                      padta hai (neeche diya hai)
  - Approval ke baad chhota "MESSAGE ID" (jaise 35143, lamba Template ID nahi)
    note karo aur portal ke WhatsApp tab me daalo.
